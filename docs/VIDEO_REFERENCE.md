# AUTUMN DRIVE: Video Technical Reference

Source: uploaded `MiniMaxH300124.mp4`.

The reference is used for visual language, not copied literally.

## Camera
- Low road-level chase/follow perspective.
- Strong forward vanishing point.
- Smooth camera movement.
- Vehicle remains readable while the environment moves.
- Motion should follow actual vehicle heading.

## Rendering
- Dark wet asphalt.
- Strong warm reflections.
- Cool gray atmospheric sky.
- Warm orange autumn vegetation.
- High local contrast around the road and vehicle.
- Subtle atmospheric depth.

## Motion
- Forward motion is communicated by road flow.
- Foreground receives more motion treatment than distant elements.
- Vehicle stays comparatively crisp.
- Avoid camera shake.

## Gameplay translation
The prototype should evolve toward:
1. Vehicle handling
2. Chase camera
3. Curved road generation
4. Wet materials/reflections
5. Autumn environment
6. Traffic and obstacles
7. Larger procedural road/world system
